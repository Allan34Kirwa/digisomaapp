# DigiSoma

**Africa's children shouldn't just use technology. They should build it.**

DigiSoma ("soma" means "learn" in Swahili) is a fun, safe learning app where children aged 6 to 16 learn coding, AI, online safety and emotional wellbeing through short lessons, games and stories rooted in African life.

**Live preview:** https://allan34kirwa.github.io/digisomaapp/

## What's inside
- **120-lesson curriculum:** 6 worlds × 4 units × 5 lessons (about 36 weeks at 3–4 lessons a week)
- **DigiSoma Lab:** 8 build missions across Kenya where children build real AI tools and hardware (drawing recogniser, decision tree, duka chatbot, catch predictor, scam guard, solar lamp circuit, smart farm watering, AI ethics council)
- **Six worlds:** Code & AI Lab, Math Arena, Cyber Shield, Mind & Heart, Growing Up, Hardware Hub
- **Akili** the robot buddy ("akili" means intelligence in Swahili) and **Soma**, a private, rule-based helper chat bot
- **Animated mini-videos** with captions and narration
- **Three age groups:** Sprouts (6–8), Builders (9–12), Pioneers (13–16)
- **Games:** robot coding with loops, Teach the AI, Teach Akili kind words (a real text classifier), Prompt power, Can AI be wrong?, Real or fake?, Scam or safe?, password builder, quizzes
- **AI curriculum** organised around the Five Big Ideas in AI (AI4K12)
- **Certificates** for every world completed
- **Create:** Akili's Story maker and a shareable Maze maker
- **Parent Zone:** PIN-protected progress, minutes learned, a weekly report and a family AI agreement
- **Sunrise (default) and night savanna themes**, large text and an easy-read font
- **Book-style front page** on phones: swipe sideways page by page

## Status
Early prototype. Progress is saved only in the browser, and payments are not live.

Built in Nairobi by Allan, an AI & Data Engineer using AI and data for social change.
